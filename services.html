Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a quote?

The fastest way is WhatsApp. Tap any "Enquire on WhatsApp" button, tell us what you need, and we'll reply with options and pricing — usually within minutes during working hours.

Which destinations do you cover?

Worldwide. Our most popular are Dubai/UAE, Turkey, Maldives, Thailand, Malaysia, Azerbaijan, Europe and Umrah to Saudi Arabia — but we can arrange trips almost anywhere.

Do you help with visa documents?

Yes. We advise on the exact requirements, review your documents before submission and handle the application to reduce the chance of rejection.

Can I customise a holiday package?

Absolutely. Every package is flexible — change the hotel, dates, duration or activities and we'll re-quote to match your budget.

How can I pay?

We offer convenient payment options and, on many bookings, flexible instalment plans. We'll confirm the details when you book.

Not sure which service you need?

Just tell us where you want to go — we'll suggest the best option and price it for you.
