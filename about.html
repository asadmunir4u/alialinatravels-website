Who we are

Making travel simple, honest and stress-free

Ali & Alina Travel & Tours is a Muscat-based travel agency built on one idea: booking a trip should feel easy, not overwhelming.

From tourist visas and air tickets to complete holiday packages and Umrah, we take care of every detail. Our travellers reach us the way that suits them best — and most love that they can plan an entire trip over WhatsApp, with quick, honest answers at every step.

Whether it's a family holiday to Dubai, a honeymoon in the Maldives, a Europe tour or a spiritual journey to the holy cities, we bring the same care, competitive pricing and personal attention to every booking.
