Top 6 Holiday Destinations from Oman in 2026

Home · Blog · Top Destinations 2026
📅 23 September 2026⏱ 6 min read✍️ Ali & Alina Travel & Tours

Muscat is one of the best-connected cities in the Gulf, which means an unforgettable holiday is only a short flight away. Whether you want beaches, culture, mountains or shopping, here are the six destinations our travellers from Oman love most in 2026 — and how to book each one in minutes on WhatsApp.

1. Dubai & the UAE — the easy weekend escape

Just a short hop from Muscat, Dubai is perfect for a long weekend or a family week away. Think record-breaking skylines, desert safaris, world-class shopping and theme parks that keep every age group happy. It's ideal for first-time travellers because everything is close, modern and easy to get around.

Great for: families, first-timers, shopping and city breaks. Ask us for a Dubai package →

2. Turkey — history, balloons and two continents

Turkey packs an incredible amount into one trip: the mosques and bazaars of Istanbul, the fairy-tale valleys and sunrise hot-air balloons of Cappadocia, and the white travertine terraces of Pamukkale. It's a favourite for travellers who want culture, scenery and value all in one itinerary.

Great for: couples, families, culture and photography. Ask us for a Turkey tour →

3. Maldives — the honeymoon dream

If you're planning a honeymoon or a special anniversary, few places match the Maldives. Overwater villas, turquoise lagoons and house reefs full of marine life make it the ultimate switch-off. It's closer to Oman than most people realise, so you spend more time relaxing and less time flying.

Great for: honeymoons, couples, diving and total relaxation. Ask us for a Maldives escape →

4. Thailand — beaches and buzzing cities

Thailand gives you two holidays in one: the temples, markets and street food of Bangkok, and the islands and beaches of Phuket and Krabi. It's excellent value, endlessly friendly and works just as well for couples as it does for groups of friends.

Great for: couples, groups, beaches and food lovers. Ask us for a Thailand combo →

5. Azerbaijan — the underrated gem

Baku surprises everyone. Its flame-shaped towers and modern skyline sit next to a beautifully preserved old city, and just outside the capital you'll find mountains, mud volcanoes and green valleys. It's close, affordable and still feels off the beaten path — perfect for travellers who've "done" the usual spots.

Great for: short breaks, nature, couples and explorers. Ask us for an Azerbaijan trip →

6. Georgia — mountains, wine and old towns

Green, dramatic and wonderfully welcoming, Georgia is made for road trips. Cobbled Tbilisi, the mountain villages of Kazbegi and some of the world's oldest wine country make it a brilliant summer escape from the Gulf heat.

Great for: nature lovers, families, road trips and summer. Ask us for a Georgia tour →
